Beat Bash Bonanza

An original browser rhythm-party series, built to a Nintendo first-party bar

18% Wave 1 — integrated, review pending 3 of 17 pieces through review
Wave 1 landed ~40 modules and the game now boots as a real product: an animated title with cast and crowd, seven original synthesised tracks, a house art style with post-processing, a layered VFX system and a procedural character rig. The agent run was cut short by a monthly spend limit part-way through, so the pieces are integrated and building but have not yet been through their critic round, and the five minigames are still placeholders.
