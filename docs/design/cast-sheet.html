The cast gets faces.

Today every Blender body is the same faceless mannequin in one flat colour with one primitive on its head. This sheet proposes what each of the eight becomes: a big head shell with a real face, a body shell that matches the menu portrait's shape, and a five-role colour split — all hung on the existing Mixamo skeleton, so every animation still works. Approve or redline the lettered decisions at the bottom.

Lineup at game scale

Heights in head units (the ruler at left). Tall build 3.4 heads, wide and round 3.0, small 2.5. Every silhouette must read on its own from this distance — that is the Swing Kings batter's size on screen.

Turnarounds

Face states — the face is a readout

One face rig for all eight: eye whites, pupils, two brows, a mouth with an open shape key. Games drive the state; Chomp Chorus uses GULP, every verdict uses the other three.

How it builds

Skeleton

Unchanged Mixamo rig. Head shell and face parts skin 100% to mixamorigHead; body shell to mixamorigSpine2; the mannequin head is hidden under the shell.

Materials

Five slots per body — body, trim, skin, accent, eye — mapped at runtime onto the house toon material with the colours below, plus the unused inverted-hull outline.

Budget

≤ 20k triangles and ≤ 6 draw calls per character (today 16.6k / 1–2; the old toy rig was 22). Shadow casting stays whole-body.

Game hooks

getJoint('face') returns a real face node; a mouthOpen shape key and brow bones replace the toy rig's mouth/gape scaling, so Chomp Chorus and the other three games can move onto these bodies.

Colours

Derived exactly as the game already does (paletteForChar): trim = body mixed 62% toward #100818, skin = 80% toward white, eye = 88% toward #06030c.

Decisions to approve

  1. A

    Big head shells, ~1/3 of height. Chibi proportions over the realistic mannequin head — faces must read at batter size. Alternative: keep the mannequin head and add a face decal (cheaper, reads far smaller).

  2. B

    Body shells follow the portrait shapes. Beak, spike, star and blob finally exist in 3D, so menu portrait and 3D figure are one character.

  3. C

    Split the doubled crests. BOPP and NIBB both had one antenna; KWARK and FIZZ both a plume. Proposed: NIBB gets twin antennae, FIZZ a star-tipped plume.

  4. D

    One outfit read each, in trim. Shoes and one garment (band, jacket, scarf, plate, hoodie, lanyard, collar, cape) — no textures, flat toon colour only.

  5. E

    Same face rig for all eight. Personality comes from default brow angle and mouth shape, not eight different rigs.

  6. F

    Toon outline on. Wire the existing inverted-hull outline for the cast only, matching the portraits' dark stroke.
